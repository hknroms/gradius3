▶ Recommended way to test
  1. Take notes as you play. Voice input on your phone is handy: just talk while you play (see "Voice input" below).
  2. Try it with invincibility first. Turn INVINCIBLE on in the menu so you can see everything to the end. Note anything that looks wrong right away.
  3. To take a picture, pause first. Hold START for about 1 second for the pause menu. Write "took a photo" in your notes so we know which photo goes with which note.
  4. When you finish, paste your notes into the report as they are and attach the photos. No need to tidy up the text. If your notes cover several things, choose "Several things at once (voice notes, etc.)" as the type. We will split them up.
  5. Sections with no problems are useful too (e.g. "Stage 5 was fine to the end").
  6. Tell us whether you played on real hardware or MAME (and the MAME version). The form has a field for this.

What to note

  • Which stage and where
  • How you started (stage and area chosen in the menu, mode)
  • What you did (turned on invincibility, what you turned on in the pause menu, etc.)
  • The position: turn POS DISP on in the pause menu and the position appears at the top right (under RANK) like X:1CA0 Y:0000. Write the X value, and the Y value too if it is not 0. This lets us reproduce the scene quickly.
  • What happened, and what should have happened

Voice input

We recommend the microphone button in the text box of the ChatGPT app. It turns what you say into text and cleans it up a little.

  1. First, send this message. The app will fix mishearings as it goes and give you a tidy summary at the end.
    I will send notes from a video game test play by voice input, in several messages. Please fix mishearings, slips and typos to fit the context of the game, and keep them in mind. Until I finish, just reply "OK". When I send "summarize", organize all the notes as text, in order, making clear the stage, place, what I did and what happened. Where I said "took a photo", write "(photo)" at that point.
  2. Tap the microphone (circled in red) and talk. The blue round button (wave shape) at the right end is "voice mode" (a spoken conversation); don't use it here.
  3. When you finish talking, tap ■ (stop). After a moment it becomes text.
  4. When the text appears, tap ↑ (send).
  5. Sent. Repeat steps 2 to 4 each time you notice something, until you finish playing.
  6. At the end, send "summarize" and paste the result into your report.
  • The button's shape and position vary a little between app versions. (The screenshots show the Japanese app.)
▶ Setting up MAME

Recommended MAME

  • The latest MAMEUI (Windows): confirmed to work. We recommend this.

Did not work in our tests: the latest official MAME for Windows / MAME4droid on phones (does not start the usual way)

Making and placing the ROM zip

  1. Make the zip with "Make a zip for MAME" on the patch page (keep the recommended set, gradius3j).
  2. Do not unzip it. Leave it in your downloads folder.
  3. In MAMEUI, add that folder to the ROM search paths (easier to replace with new versions than moving the zip): "Options" → "Directories" → choose "ROMs" at the top → "Insert" → pick the downloads folder → "OK".
  4. Refresh the list (F5). Gradius III (Japan, version 3) appears. Double-click to start.
When you make a zip for a new version, overwrite the old one. If a file with the same name exists, it is saved as "gradius3j (1).zip" and MAME ignores it (you keep playing the old version). Delete the old zip first, or rename the new one to gradius3j.zip.

After starting

  • MAME warns about incorrect checksums. This is normal for a modified ROM; press any key to continue.
  • Right after power-on, the version and build time appear under "RAM ROM CHECK" (e.g. 0 4 17 26 10 26 10:12). Including it in reports tells us exactly which version you used.
  • Insert a coin: 5 / 1P start: 1 (default keys)

Button mapping

  • During the game press Tab → "Input (this Machine)" → select an item, press Enter, then press the button you want.
  • If a controller such as a PS5 pad doesn't respond, remap it there, or try DS4Windows or Steam Input.

If MAME says the ROM is missing

  • Is the zip named exactly gradius3j.zip (no "(1)")?
  • Is its folder listed under ROMs in "Directories"?
  • Did you pick "Gradius III (Japan, version 3)"? If you made the gradius3 set instead, pick "World, version R".
